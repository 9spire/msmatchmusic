Biography

Moving bodies & minds is what she does best.

Ms Match brings an obsession-level focus to selecting, combining, and creating the music that keeps her and the crowd moving. Behind the decks, she channels the universal joy of losing oneself on the dance floor.

Background

Based in Portland, Oregon, Ms Match has played her signature style of vocal-driven, hip-hop influenced breakbeat, tech house, and bass house at festivals and venues across the region.

She produces as Luv Freq, with original releases on Spotify — including the album 22 45 and the Drop It EP featuring a Marcus Funkhouser remix.
